Hybrid System Deep Dive

Toyota e-CVT (RAV4, Corolla Cross, C-HR)

Parallel hybrid with planetary gearset. Electric only at low speed, engine at cruise, both on acceleration. No traditional gearbox — essentially maintenance-free. 20+ years proven, battery failure rate <0.1%. 8yr/160k km warranty.

Kia/Hyundai 6-Speed (Sportage, Tucson)

1.6L turbo + 6-speed auto + 44.9kW e-motor. More traditional feel, can cruise electrically. Same engine as ICE versions. 7yr warranty (Kia), 5yr (Hyundai).

Nissan e-POWER (Qashqai)

Petrol engine NEVER drives wheels — acts only as generator for 140kW electric motor. Feels like EV. Simpler drivetrain but less efficient on motorways (7.5-8.5 L/100km). Generator noise noticeable on hard acceleration.

📋 Buying Guide

Step-by-Step

  1. Define priorities — Rear space > boot space > reliability > warranty
  2. Shortlist 2-3 models — Sportage GT-Line, Tucson Elite, or RAV4 Design
  3. Search listings — CarsIreland.ie, Carzone.ie, DoneDeal.ie
  4. Verify HEV — Confirm full HEV (not MHEV/PHEV) on registration docs
  5. Service history — Full dealer service history essential
  6. Pre-purchase inspection — CarVertical or mechanic (€50-100)
  7. Test drive — Test EV mode, hybrid transitions, infotainment
  8. Warranty check — Kia 7yr transferable. Ask dealer to confirm.
  9. Negotiate — Start 5-10% below asking. End-of-month is best.
  10. Arrange finance — Compare multiple providers, don't accept dealer only
  11. NCT — Check validity. Factor €80 cost if due soon
  12. Complete purchase — V5C assignment, receipt, insurance, motor tax

🔍 Used Hybrid Checklist

✅ Good Signs

  • 📋 Full dealer service history
  • 🔋 Battery health report
  • 📝 Transferable warranty
  • 🚗 Low mileage (<15k km/yr)
  • ⚡ Smooth transitions
  • 🔌 No warning lights

⚠️ Red Flags

  • ❌ No service history
  • 🚨 Check engine light
  • 🔊 Rough transitions
  • 📉 Mileage inconsistent
  • 💸 Price too low
  • 🚗 Multiple owners
  • 📋 NCT due soon

💡 Tips

Best Time to Buy: End of month/quarter. December/January often have better deals.
Where to Look: CarsIreland.ie (largest), Carzone.ie (good filters), DoneDeal.ie (private, cheapest), Facebook Marketplace
Negotiation: Mention competing listings. If NCT due within 3 months, negotiate €80-120 off. End-of-month is strongest.
Avoid: MHEV (not full hybrid), no service history, cars under finance, European imports without NCT/VRT clearance
